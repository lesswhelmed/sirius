# Sirius — Brand Strategy

_The foundational document for the Sirius brand: thesis, positioning, architecture, and the north star. Last updated 2026-06-29._

> **Sirius is the life-context engine.** Everything you capture becomes private context about your life — your aspirations, projects, and inputs — and that context is what lets Sirius close the loop, from scattered inputs to drafts to organization to action, on your terms.

---

## I. Brand thesis

Most people are drowning in their own inputs. Links they meant to read. Screenshots they took and forgot. Notes that never became anything. Voice memos lost in a thread. The tools we use to capture all this are good at storing and bad at understanding. Nothing connects. Nothing compounds.

Sirius is built on a different premise: **the things you capture are not clutter — they are context about your life.** Treated that way, they can do real work. A link becomes a clean, structured finding. A batch of photos becomes a kept record. A note becomes a draft worth keeping. And over time, the corpus of everything you've captured becomes a map of who you are and what you're working toward — the substrate that lets the system organize for you and, increasingly, act for you.

That is the wedge. Competitors store your stuff. **Sirius understands it, and closes the loop.**

### Positioning statement (the one-liner)

For the broad prosumer drowning in scattered inputs, **Sirius is the life-context engine** that turns anything you capture into something worth keeping — and uses the accumulated context to organize, surface, and close the loop on what matters. Unlike second-brain and note tools that only store and recall, Sirius **understands your context and acts on it, on your terms.**

## II. Brand architecture

There is **one hero brand: Sirius.** It is the name of both the product and the company.

| Element | Canonical form | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company | **Sirius** | The maker. |
| Product | **Sirius** | The app (iOS + web). |
| App Store name | **Sirius** | Listing title. |
| Wordmark | **Sirius** | Always capitalized, never stylized to "SIRIUS" or "sirius" in body copy. |
| Domain | **lesswhelmed.com** | The web home and email domain. |
| Email | name@lesswhelmed.com | Correspondence. |
| Emotional promise | **"Feel lesswhelmed."** | The payoff, not a brand. |

### Where "lesswhelmed" lives — and where it doesn't

`lesswhelmed` survives in **exactly two places**:

1. The **web domain** (lesswhelmed.com) and the email domain.
2. The **emotional promise / payoff** — the feeling Sirius delivers: "Feel lesswhelmed."

It is **never a co-brand.** We do not write "Sirius by Lesswhelmed", "Sirius — a Lesswhelmed product", or "Lesswhelmed Sirius". The word is lowercase, always. It is a feeling and a URL, not a logo.

**Rationale.** A single hero name is easier to remember, defend, and grow. "Sirius" carries meaning (a fixed navigational star — see below); "lesswhelmed" carries feeling. Keeping them in their lanes gives us a name to own and a promise to deliver without the dilution of a two-headed brand.

## III. The name and the symbol

### Sirius — the star

**Sirius is the brightest star in the night sky.** For millennia, sailors steered by it — a fixed point you could navigate against when everything else was dark and moving. That is the brand meaning we lean on: **clarity, and a fixed point amid overwhelm.** When your inputs feel like chaos, Sirius is the thing that holds still and tells you where you are.

### The cat — the knowing companion

The app icon is a **black cat with luminous sage-gold eyes on a midnight-navy ground, peeking out of a black bag.** The cat is a quiet motif, not a mascot to over-explain. Lean on three meanings, lightly:

- **A cat sees in the dark.** It finds signal in your clutter — the way Sirius finds the keepable thing in the pile.
- **A cat is curious.** It goes looking; it notices.
- **"Letting the cat out of the bag."** Revealing what matters — pulling the one thing worth keeping out of the noise.

The **star is the guide**; the **cat is the knowing, nocturnal companion** that does the seeing. Use the cat as atmosphere and warmth. Never caption it, never anthropomorphize it into a spokes-character.

## IV. Brand pillars

Four pillars hold up everything Sirius says and ships.

### 1. Capture anything, keep something worth it
The universal hook. Links, photos, screenshots, notes, code repos, YouTube videos, documents, voice memos — in via the Capture tab or the iOS Share Sheet, with no app-switching. Every capture is drafted into a clean, structured post you review before keeping. **"Capture now. Sorted later."**

### 2. Context that compounds
Every capture is private context about your life. The more you put in, the more Sirius understands your aspirations, projects, and focus areas — and the better it maps, connects, and surfaces what matters. This is the **moat**: context accumulates, and a competitor starting from zero can't replicate yours.

### 3. Your data, your terms
Private by default. Every original is retained for you, not mined. On-device drafting on iPhone means some AI work never leaves your hands. One-tap full export. No ads, no cross-app tracking. You expose your corpus to outside AI tools only if **you** choose to (the MCP corpus server).

### 4. You decide what's kept
Sirius drafts; you approve. AI does the tedious shaping — title, summary, tags, categories, body — and then steps back. **"AI drafts it. You decide what's worth keeping."** Nothing is published or kept behind your back.

## V. Brand personality and archetype

**Archetype:** the **Sage** with a quiet streak of the **Magician.** Sirius is the calm, well-read companion who helps you see clearly — with just enough quiet magic in how it transforms a mess of inputs into something ordered and useful.

**Personality, in five words:** calm, perceptive, editorial, trustworthy, quietly confident.

**Voice in one breath:** plain-spoken and editorial, like a good newspaper. It gives peace of mind, so the copy should feel like peace of mind. Concrete verbs over abstractions. Short sentences. No hype.

## VII. The north star (roadmap — not yet shipped)

The arc is simple: **build Life Context, then close the Loop.** These phases are the destination, not today's product. Never describe them as shipped.

1. **Aspiration Core.** Capture the user's aspirations, projects, and focus areas so the system knows _why_ a captured thing matters. The seed of Life Context.
2. **Universal Collector.** Frictionless capture from everywhere. _Largely shipped today._
3. **Insight Engine.** An Opportunity Dashboard, "Connect-the-Dots" relationship surfacing, and research that scans your **entire** private corpus.
4. **Automation Engine — closing the Loop.** One-click suggested actions and if-this-then-that Automations that act on your behalf, on your terms.

### The moat

**Life Context compounds.** The longer you use Sirius, the more it knows your goals and the better it closes the loop. Competitors store; **Sirius understands and acts.** This is durable because it isn't a feature — it's the accumulated, private, per-user context that only time and use can build.

---

## VIII. Category context

Sirius sits in the **personal context / memory layer** category that is being called the defining AI capability of 2026 — the shift from systems that _respond_ to systems that _remember_ to systems that _ambiently assist_. The roadmap (ambient agents that close the loop) is squarely in the proactive, context-aware direction the field is moving. The market for personal knowledge-base AI is real and growing fast (~30% CAGR), and every credible competitor sells capture and recall — **none own the context layer that turns a second brain into a system that acts.** That gap is ours.
