# Sirius — Prelaunch Social Playbook

_The founder-led social drip for the weeks BEFORE launch (GTM weeks −6→−1). Companion to [`04-launch-announcement.md`](./04-launch-announcement.md), which holds the launch-DAY copy (the X thread, Product Hunt, Show HN, etc.). Voice per [`brand/01-messaging-framework.md`](../brand/01-messaging-framework.md). Last updated 2026-07-02._

**Job of this doc:** warm the audience and fill the waitlist before the [launch week](./03-go-to-market-plan.md#v-timeline) beats. Build-in-public, founder-first. Every claim honors the **honesty rule** — sell the shipped joy, never present automation/closing-the-loop as live.

## The attract → convert model (applies to every post)
- **Attract:** make them feel seen, or show an undeniable demo (curiosity gap + motion). A visual is mandatory — a tweet with a demo GIF gets 5–10× the reach.
- **Convert:** exactly one CTA (waitlist link, or "reply/DM for beta"), one reason to act now (scarce TestFlight beta), and rising social proof. No post asks for two things.

**Defaults:** CTA = waitlist at `lesswhelmed.com` + a limited **TestFlight beta** ("first 100") as the act-now lever. Voice = founder personal (build-in-public) with brand lines for amplification. Cadence = 1/day through Phases 1–3, 2/day in launch week; best slots 9am + 1pm ET; ≤1 hashtag (`#buildinpublic`) on founder posts only.

**Assets** live in `marketing-gifs/` — produce/re-cut them with the [`scripts/marketing/`](../../../scripts/marketing/README.md) toolkit (record → GIF → App Store preview).

## Phase 1 — Tension (make them feel seen) · ~weeks −6 to −4

**① Thesis (pin this)**
> You capture more than you'll ever keep.
> The link you meant to read. The screenshot you forgot. The note that died in a thread.
>
> We're building **Sirius**: capture anything, and AI drafts it into something worth keeping.
> Your life, in context. → lesswhelmed.com

*Asset: hero — messy grid of tabs/screenshots resolving into 3 clean posts. Attracts: names a universal pain in one line. Converts: soft waitlist CTA; becomes the pinned anchor.*

**② The gut-punch**
> open tabs: 47
> screenshots you'll never open again: ~900
> notes that start "idea:" and go nowhere: countless
>
> You don't have a memory problem. You have a **context** problem.

*Asset: 4-sec screen-record of a chaotic camera roll. Attracts: hyper-relatable, quotable, no CTA (pure reach). Converts: reframes the category → sets up Sirius as the answer.*

**③ Contrarian wedge**
> Everyone's shipping "AI memory."
> Memory is table stakes — a flat list a vendor owns.
> The hard part isn't remembering. It's understanding **why** a thing matters to *you*.
> That's what we're building. 🐈‍⬛

*Asset: brand text card. Attracts: a POV that invites debate = replies = distribution. Converts: plants the context-layer differentiator early.*

## Phase 2 — Show the magic (demos convert best) · ~weeks −4 to −2

**④ The core wow — capture → draft → keep**
> Share a link into Sirius. Type nothing.
> Seconds later it's a clean post — title, summary, tags — waiting for you to keep it.
> No blank page. No filing. (You always review first.)

*Asset: `marketing-gifs/capture-share-to-catalog.gif` (Safari → Share Sheet → Sirius → Queued → Catalog). Attracts: the "wait, what?" moment; motion stops the scroll. Converts: the demo IS the pitch → waitlist.*

**⑤ Talk to your library**
> New: you can now **talk to** everything you've captured.
> "What have I saved about pricing?" → a real answer, from *your own posts*, with the sources cited.
> Not the web. Not a vendor's memory. Yours.

*Asset: (record fresh) Catalog → Ask your library → streamed cited answer. Attracts: chat-with-your-stuff is instantly graspable + timely. Converts: proves "context that compounds" is real, today.*

**⑥ The map**
> Your captures aren't clutter. They're context.
> Sirius maps your whole library by meaning — so "that thing about X" is findable even when you've forgotten the words.
> (yes, it's also a little 3D galaxy. we couldn't resist.)

*Asset: `marketing-gifs/observatory-map.gif`. Attracts: visually gorgeous = shares. Converts: differentiates from flat notes apps.*

**⑦ Search that reads your mind**
> Search by **meaning**, not keywords.
> Type "quiet coastal mornings" → get the sunrise photo, the note, and the article — even if none of them say those words.

*Asset: (record fresh) Catalog search → vibe phrase → exact/similar badges. Attracts: concrete "it just gets it." Converts: reinforces daily-use value.*

## Phase 3 — Trust & build-in-public · ~week −2 to −1

**⑧ Privacy is the default**
> Your data should be yours.
> On iPhone, Sirius can draft your captures **entirely on-device** — no server round-trip.
> Private by default. No ads. No cross-app tracking. One-tap export of everything.
> Not an upsell. The default.

*Asset: (record fresh) airplane-mode drafting. Attracts: privacy is a values-magnet for the second-brain crowd. Converts: dissolves the "training on my data?" objection preemptively.*

**⑨ Radical honesty (this itself converts)**
> What Sirius does **today**: capture anything, draft it, organize it, map it by meaning, make it searchable — all private.
> What it does **not** do yet: act for you.
> Closing the loop — suggestions that take action — is the north star. We'll say "shipped" when it's shipped.

*Asset: two-column Today / North-star card. Attracts: honesty is rare + screenshot-worthy in an overclaiming category. Converts: builds the trust that earns the email.*

**⑩ Founder "why"**
> I built Sirius because I was drowning in my own inputs.
> ~1,000 screenshots. Links I'd never reopen. A "someday" folder that was really a graveyard.
> I didn't want another notes app. I wanted the pile to finally **mean** something.
> Building in public from here. 🐈‍⬛

*Asset: real desk/founder shot or the "graveyard" folder. Attracts: origin stories are the most-followed BIP format. Converts: turns a product into a person to root for → follows → launch-day buyers.*

## Phase 4 — FOMO & CTA ramp · launch week (feeds into [`04`](./04-launch-announcement.md))

**⑪ Scarce beta**
> First **100** people on the waitlist get early TestFlight this week.
> Capture anything. Get back something worth keeping. Then tell us what breaks.
> Reply "in" or grab a spot → lesswhelmed.com

*Asset: `marketing-gifs/note-to-audience.gif` or TestFlight screen. Attracts: scarcity + a job ("tell us what breaks" = ownership). Converts: the hardest CTA — a number, a deadline, a link.*

**⑫ Countdown + social proof**
> **[X]** people on the Sirius waitlist and counting.
> iOS + web. Private by default. Your life, in context.
> Next TestFlight batch opens Friday → lesswhelmed.com

*Asset: waitlist counter / a beta-tester quote. Attracts: momentum. Converts: recurring, swappable [X]/day post that manufactures a bandwagon into launch.*

> **Launch day itself:** pin the [X launch thread](./04-launch-announcement.md#x-thread), then run the PH / Show HN / LinkedIn sequence per the [timeline](./03-go-to-market-plan.md#v-timeline).
