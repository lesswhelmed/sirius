---

## What it is

Sirius is the **life-context engine**: a native iOS + web app that turns everything
you capture — links, photos, notes, code, videos, documents, even voice memos — into
private, structured context about your life, then organizes, maps, and searches it.
On iPhone, the AI drafting can run **entirely on-device**. You always review before
anything is kept.

## Who it's for

Broad prosumer. Lead with the universal hook — *capture anything, get something
worth keeping* — and let segments self-select:

- **The overwhelmed professional** drowning in tabs, screenshots, and half-thoughts.
- **The second-brain power user** who's outgrown flat notes and bookmark piles.
- **The creator** turning a stream of inputs into publishable, organized output.

## The problem

We capture more than ever and understand less of it. Notes apps are passive
drawers. Bookmark apps hoard URLs. Chatbot "memory" is a flat list owned by a
vendor. None of them know **why** a captured thing matters to *you* — so nothing
ever closes the loop from scattered input to useful action.

## The wedge

Everyone is building a **memory layer**. Memory is table stakes. Sirius owns the
**context layer** that turns a second brain into a system that *acts* — a private,
compounding graph of your aspirations, projects, and inputs, with ownership and
on-device privacy as defaults, not upsells.

## Key capabilities (ships today)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capture anything** | Capture tab + iOS Share Sheet — no app-switching |
| **AI drafting** | Schema-validated Gemini pipeline; on-device option on iPhone |
| **Auto-classified posts** | FINDING (URLs), SIGHT (images), BLOG (notes) + Instagram/YouTube |
| **Catalog + Queue** | Private-by-default library; live pending → done state machine |
| **The Tree / Observatory** | Your corpus mapped by meaning: taxonomy + 3D embedding space |
| **Friends + shared feed** | Per-post PRIVATE / FRIENDS / GROUP / PUBLIC; named groups |
| **Curated search** | Full-text + embedding similarity, RRF-fused, visibility-gated |
| **Your data, your terms** | Originals retained privately; one-tap full export |
| **MCP corpus server** | Expose your corpus to the AI agents you choose, via per-user key |

## Proof

- Native iOS app + web app live at **lesswhelmed.com**; Android build exists in EAS.
- AI drafting produces **schema-validated** posts — no brittle hand-parsed JSON.
- **On-device drafting** on iPhone (local llama.rn model): private, no round-trip.
- **Full export** of every retained original, emailed as a download link.
- **No third-party ads, no cross-app tracking.**
