### Description

**Your life, in context.**

Sirius turns the scattered stuff of your life into context you can use. Capture a
link, a photo, a screenshot, a note, a code repo, a YouTube video, a document, or a
voice memo — and Sirius drafts it into a clean, structured post you can keep, find,
and build on. You always review before anything is saved.

**Capture anything, no app-switching**
Use the Capture tab or share straight from any app into Sirius with the iOS Share
Sheet.

**AI drafts it — you decide**
Each capture becomes a post with a title, summary, tags, categories, and body. On
iPhone, drafting can run entirely on-device — private, no server round-trip. Review
every draft before it's kept.

**Organized automatically**
Captures are auto-classified and filed into your catalog, private by default. A live
queue means nothing gets lost.

**Mapped by meaning**
The Tree shows your whole corpus as a taxonomy outline and a 3D space, grouping what
belongs together so you can see the shape of what you know.

**Search that understands**
Find anything with combined full-text and similarity search — even when you can't
remember the exact words.

**Share on your terms**
Keep posts private, share with friends, share to a named group, or make them public.
A shared feed, separate from your private catalog.

**Your data, your terms**
Every original you upload is retained privately and kept until you delete it. Export
everything in one tap. No third-party ads. No cross-app tracking.

**Feel lesswhelmed.**

_Sirius offers a free tier with a weekly AI-draft limit and an optional Pro
subscription. Web companion at lesswhelmed.com._
