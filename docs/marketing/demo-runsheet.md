# Sirius — Live Demo Runsheet

_Glance-and-go script for presenting the app. Bold = what you DO. "Quotes" = what you SAY._
_Total ≈ 5 min. A 30-sec version is at the bottom._

---

## ⏱ Before you start (2 min, off-stage)
- [ ] Phone on **Do Not Disturb**, brightness up, screen-record ready if capturing.
- [ ] Logged in to the **demo account** (seeded — Catalog/Tree/search look full).
- [ ] Pre-pick **one article** (in Safari) + **one photo** (in Photos) to share.
- [ ] Have **one capture already "done"** in the Queue (in case live drafting is slow).
- [ ] Web open in a tab at **lesswhelmed.com** → the **Observatory** (`/space`), for the big-screen moment.
- [ ] Wi-Fi check. If using **on-device drafting**, confirm the model is downloaded.

---

## 🎬 The opening line (10 sec)
> "We all capture way more than we ever use — links, screenshots, voice notes that die in a thread. Sirius turns any of it into something worth keeping. Watch."

---

## 1️⃣ Capture anything — the Share Sheet (the hook) — 45 sec
**Open Safari → an article → tap Share → pick Sirius.**
> "I'm reading something. Instead of bookmarking it to never see again, I share it straight into Sirius — no app-switching."

**Switch to Photos → pick a photo → Share → Sirius.**
> "Same with a photo or a screenshot."

**(Optional) In the Capture tab, tap the mic → say one line → stop.**
> "Or I just talk to it."

✅ *Point made: capture is two taps, from anywhere.*

---

## 2️⃣ The magic moment — AI drafts it — 45 sec
**Open the Queue tab.** Show the row(s) moving **pending → processing → done**.
> "Everything I capture lands here so nothing's lost. Sirius is drafting each one right now."

**Tap the finished post.** Show the **title, summary, tags, body**.
> "That messy link came back as a clean post — title, summary, tags — written for me."

**(If on-device) point to the on-device badge.**
> "On iPhone this can run entirely on-device. The capture never leaves my phone."

✅ *If live drafting lags: open the pre-made "done" post instead. Keep talking.*

---

## 3️⃣ You stay in control — 15 sec
**Show the review/keep action on the draft.**
> "AI only drafts. I decide what's worth keeping. Nothing is ever published behind my back."

---

## ⭐ Power beat — the "Full analysis" (best with a video) — 45 sec
_Use a **YouTube capture** for this — it's the "wow." Have one pre-drafted and ready._

**Open a video post → expand the "Full analysis" section.**
> "For a video, Sirius goes deeper. It doesn't just summarize — watch."

**Scroll the blocks: Overview → Key claims → Tools & frameworks → Notable moments → Quotes → Entities.**
> "An overview, the key claims, every tool it named, the notable moments, verbatim quotes, and all the people, places, and concepts — pulled out automatically."

**Tap a timestamp on a Notable moment or Quote.**
> "And every moment is a deep-link — one tap jumps me to that exact spot in the video."

✅ *Scope it honestly: this six-block Full analysis is for **videos**. Don't imply every capture gets it.*
✅ *If short on time, skip this beat — it's the upgrade, not the core loop.*

---

## 4️⃣ It compounds — Catalog + the Tree — 60 sec
**Open the Catalog.** Scroll the library.
> "Every keeper lives here — my private library, organized automatically."

**Open the Tree tab (mobile) — or switch to the web Observatory on the big screen.**
> "And here's the part no notes app does: my whole corpus, mapped by *meaning*. Related things cluster together. This is my life, in context."

**Tap a node / topic → show the posts under it.**
> "I can drill into any theme and see everything connected to it."

✅ *The web Observatory (`/space`) is the most impressive visual — use it if you have a screen.*

---

## 5️⃣ Find anything by meaning — Search — 30 sec
**Open search → type a vague phrase (not exact words).**
> "I don't have to remember what I called it. I search by meaning and it finds the right thing — exact matches and similar ideas."

---

## 6️⃣ Your data, your terms — 20 sec
**Go to Settings → show the Export / Archive option.**
> "It's all mine. One tap exports everything. No ads, no tracking."

**(For technical crowds only) mention the MCP key.**
> "Power users can even plug their own corpus into the AI tools they already use."

---

## 🧲 The closing line (10 sec)
> "So that's Sirius: capture anything, get something worth keeping, find it by meaning, and own all of it. Your life, in context. **Feel lesswhelmed.**"

---

## 🛟 If something breaks (stay calm, keep moving)
- **Live draft is slow / fails** → open the **pre-made finished post**. "Here's one I captured earlier."
- **No Wi-Fi** → demo **on-device drafting** (no network needed) + the **Catalog/Tree** (already loaded).
- **Share Sheet doesn't show Sirius** → fall back to typing/pasting into the **Capture tab**.
- **App misbehaves** → switch to the **web app** on the big screen; same flow, bigger visual.
- **Anything empty-looking** → you're on the wrong account; switch to the **seeded demo account**.

---

## ⚡ 30-second version (elevator / hallway)
1. **Share an article into Sirius** → "Capture anything, anywhere."
2. **Open the post** → "AI drafts it into a clean, structured post — I just review."
3. **Open the Tree/Observatory** → "Everything mapped by meaning, fully private, fully mine."
> "Your life, in context. Feel lesswhelmed."

---

## 🚫 Don't say (honesty rule)
- Don't claim it **automates** or **"closes the loop today"** — that's roadmap.
- Avoid *supercharge / seamless / revolutionary*. Use plain verbs: capture, draft, keep, map, find.
- Don't show an empty corpus — always demo on the seeded account.
- The six-block **Full analysis is for videos (YouTube)** — don't say "every capture gets a full analysis." (Instagram gets a different digest; links/notes/images get a clean summary.)
